Signs of real news

  • Named sources ("officials said")
  • Specific dates and numbers
  • Neutral, factual tone

Signs of fake news

  • ALL CAPS and "!!!"
  • "SHOCKING", "share this"
  • Vague claims, no named source
Limitations: this tool checks writing style and source presence, not factual truth. The model was trained on US political news from 2016-17, so style scores are less reliable for other topics and countries. "Unverified" does not mean fake: new or local news may not be indexed yet. It is a first-level filter and does not replace human fact-checking.
